# Northwind Help Center (fictional — for grounding labs)

## Refunds
- Annual plans: full refund within **14 days** of purchase; pro-rated after that.
- Duplicate charges are refunded within **5 business days** once confirmed.

## Billing
- Invoices export from **Settings → Billing → Export**.
- Cards are updated under **Settings → Billing → Payment method**.

## Accounts
- Password reset links expire after **60 minutes**; request a new one from the login page.
- Admin lockouts are cleared by another admin, or by support with identity verification.

## Seats
- Add seats under **Settings → Team → Add member**. Billing pro-rates automatically.

## Data & Compliance
- Do **not** upload regulated data (PHI/PII) to any AI feature unless your workspace has a
  signed BAA/DPA. Health records require a human-approved, compliant workflow.
