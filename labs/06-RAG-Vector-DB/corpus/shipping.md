# Northwind Shipping Policy

## Delivery times

Standard shipping arrives in **3 to 5 business days**. Expedited shipping arrives in **1 to 2
business days**. Orders placed after 2 PM ship the next business day.

## Shipping costs

Standard shipping is **free on orders over $50**. Below $50, standard shipping is a flat $5.
Expedited shipping is $15 regardless of order size.

## International

We ship to Canada and Mexico. International orders may be subject to customs duties paid by the
recipient. We do not currently ship to other countries.
