# Northwind Warranty

## Coverage

All electronics include a **one-year limited warranty** covering manufacturing defects. The
warranty does not cover accidental damage, water damage, or normal wear.

## Making a claim

To make a warranty claim, provide your order number and a description of the problem. Approved
claims are repaired or replaced within 10 business days. You pay shipping to us; we pay shipping
back.

## Extended warranty

An optional extended warranty adds two years of coverage and must be purchased within 30 days of
the original order.
